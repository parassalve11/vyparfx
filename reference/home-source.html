How do I open an account with TradeKaro?
Opening an account with TradeKaro is simple and can be done online. All you need is a Phone number and email Address, that’s it ! No other documents required. The account opening process typically takes just a few seconds.
How do I deposit funds into my TradeKaro account?

You can deposit funds into your TradeKaro account through multiple methods, including:

  • Net Banking
  • UPI
  • NEFT/RTGS
  • IMPS

Deposits are usually processed instantly or within a minutes, depending on the method used.

How can I withdraw funds from my TradeKaro account?

Withdrawing funds from your TradeKaro account is easy and can be done via our     platform. Follow these steps    

  1. Log in to your TradeKaro account.
  2. Navigate to the “Withdrawals section”
  3. Click on “New Request”
  4. Enter the amount you wish to withdraw, enter your Bank details and submit.

 

Withdrawals are typically processed Instantly or within 2 hours and will be credited to your registered bank account.
